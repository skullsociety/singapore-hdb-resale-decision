## Calculator boundaries

- It treats BSD, ABSD, renovation, legal, valuation, insurance, moving, and other fees as cash at the outset. Actual timing and any CPF reimbursement can differ. User can override each fee; zero means excluded.
- It does not calculate HFE eligibility, a bank's approved amount, CPF grants, mortgage servicing limits, ABSD remission, second HDB loan obligations, or CPF refund from selling an existing home. Enter only funds genuinely available for this purchase; Phase 3 will address selling proceeds.
- `five_year_cash_and_cpf_outflow_sgd` is a simple sum of initial cash and CPF used plus 60 monthly payments and user monthly costs. It is **not** net ownership cost: it does not deduct remaining equity or include changes in rates, property taxes, resale costs, or investment returns.
- The affordability ceiling is a screening calculation assuming HDB value equals price and the planning LTV is available. It can overstate actual affordability when cash over valuation, loan limits, or CPF caps differ.
- Model fair-value bounds can be copied from step 10 to compare with an asking price. They never replace HDB's value for financing or stamp duty.
